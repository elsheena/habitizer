About Habitizer

We believe lasting personal growth is not about fighting yourself with exhausting willpower. It is about understanding how your brain works and gently redirecting old urges into healthy, rewarding routines.

The 3 Pillars of Habitizer

1. Substitution Over Struggle

When a craving or stress trigger strikes, do not just say no. Have an easy 5-minute healthy replacement routine ready (water, deep breathing, stretching).

2. Forgiveness and Freezes

Progress is not linear. Our Streak Freeze system protects your record during stressful days so a single slip-up never resets your motivation back to zero.

3. Decoupled Architecture

Built on lightweight, independent Golang microservices with data isolation and offline support, giving you fast performance anywhere on web or mobile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Shop and currency work?

Every day you avoid or replace your bad habits, you earn 10 coins. You can spend them in the Shop to buy extra Streak Freezes or unlock guilt-free leisure screen time passes.

What is the difference between Free and Premium plans?

Free users can track up to 3 active habit substitution loops at a time. Premium users get unlimited habits, advanced habit pattern analytics, and smart Google Calendar sync.

Can I use Habitizer on my phone?

Yes. Habitizer is completely mobile-responsive with a touch-friendly navigation bar. You can open it on your phone browser or run the companion Flutter mobile app.
